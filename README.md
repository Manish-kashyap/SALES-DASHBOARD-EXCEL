🚀 Project Overview

The Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Excel business intelligence project designed to transform raw sales data into meaningful and actionable insights.

The dashboard analyzes the performance of sales executives across 8 cities and provides management with a quick overview of:

Total sales performance

Target achievement

Performance gaps

Top-performing executives

Underperforming executives

City-wise sales performance


By combining Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, KPIs, formulas, and data visualization, the dashboard creates an easy-to-use analytical reporting solution.

📊 Key Performance Indicators

💰 1. Total Sales

Total Sales represents the amount of sales generated by each sales executive.

Purpose

It helps management quickly identify employees who are contributing the most revenue.

Analysis:
Higher sales indicate stronger revenue contribution.


---

🎯 2. Target Hit %

Target Hit % measures how much of an employee's assigned sales target has been achieved.

Formula

Target Hit % =
(Achieved Sales ÷ Target Sales) × 100

Example

Anita Dixit — 63.20%

This means Anita achieved approximately 63.20% of the assigned sales target.


---

📉 3. Away From Target %

Away From Target % represents the remaining percentage required to reach the assigned target.

Formula

Away From Target % =
100% − Target Hit %

Purpose

This KPI helps management quickly identify executives who are significantly below their assigned targets.
